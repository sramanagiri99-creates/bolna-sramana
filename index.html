THE MARKETING OPPORTUNITY

Marketing today is no longer neatly divided into B2B and B2C. People discover brands through content, creators, communities, conversations and experiences.

Organic marketing builds familiarity, credibility and word of mouth over time. It creates an additional advantage beyond paid acquisition, allowing a brand to grow through the people who discover, use and talk about it.

For Bolna, the opportunity is to turn existing product momentum and word of mouth into a much stronger brand presence.

Suggestion01OF 05

MAKE VOICE THE CAMPAIGN, NOT JUST THE PRODUCT.

DON'T JUST TELL PEOPLE WHAT VOICE AI CAN DO. LET THEM EXPERIENCE IT.

Bolna has a particularly interesting marketing advantage: its product can be experienced through an actual conversation.

I'd build larger campaign concepts rather than treating every post as an isolated creative.

01.A

THE VOICE TEST

Can AI handle your accent, language, questions and interruptions? Invite people to call and test Bolna.

01.B

CALL THE FUTURE

Let audiences experience everyday interactions handled by AI voice agents.

01.C

INDIA, WHAT'S YOUR LANGUAGE?

Show how voice AI works across languages and code-switching through real conversations.

01.D

HUMAN VS AI

Put an AI agent and a human through the same task and compare the experience.

Think campaign-led brand building, taking inspiration from Zomato and CRED—not copying their communication, but their ability to turn individual ideas into memorable brand campaigns.
